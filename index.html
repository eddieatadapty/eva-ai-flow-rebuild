Day 1

Pass the campaign and keyword into Adapty

After a one-time SDK setup, the Meta campaign or the Apple Ads keyword each install came from arrives as a user attribute and picks the flow at first open. No app release.

Week 1

One flow per source

The Meta ad, your brand search and the category search each get their own first screens; everything after the paywall stays as it is.

Week 2

Judge each source on what it earns

Each flow against today’s, measured on purchases and what each install from that ad or search earns, so spend follows the promise that pays.

All three phones run the whole flow with the five ideas. On Apple Ads you hold about 46% of US searches for your own name “eva”, a brand defence with room to grow.
